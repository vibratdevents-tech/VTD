# Vibra TD Events — Design Brainstorm

## Brand Context
- Event planning company in Trou d'Eau Douce, Mauritius
- Services: Team Buildings, Sports & Leisure, Kids Entertainment, Wellness Programs, Event Décor & Set-ups
- Brand colors from logo: Teal/turquoise (#2BBCB0), warm gold/amber (#F5A623), vibrant magenta/purple (#9B59B6)
- Tagline: "We turn moments into memories"
- Energy: vibrant, community-focused, joyful, tropical island vibes

**Idea A — "Tropical Carnival Energy"**

- **Design Movement**: Neo-tropical maximalism meets editorial event branding
- **Core Principles**:
  1. Bold color blocking with tropical warmth — deep teal, golden amber, coral, and white
  2. Diagonal section cuts and asymmetric card layouts to convey movement and energy
  3. Layered depth through overlapping cards, floating badges, and staggered photo grids
  4. Handcrafted feel via organic shapes and textured backgrounds (subtle grain overlay)
- **Color Philosophy**: Deep teal (#1A8C85) as anchor, warm amber (#F5A623) as energy accent, coral (#FF6B6B) for CTAs, off-white (#FFF9F0) as warm canvas. Colors evoke Mauritius beach sunsets and tropical vibrancy.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Diagonal hero with clipped bottom edge, asymmetric two-column service cards, staggered testimonial quotes with large quotation marks, full-bleed photo strips
- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Wavy SVG dividers between sections (tropical wave motif)
  2. Circular photo frames with teal/gold border rings
  3. Bold section numbers (01, 02, 03) as decorative anchors
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Cards lift on hover with shadow depth, CTA buttons pulse subtly, scroll-triggered entrance animations for each section
- **Animation**: Staggered fade-up on scroll (60ms delay per item), hero text slides in from left, service cards scale from 0.95 to 1.0 on enter
- **Typography System**: Display — Playfair Display (bold, italic for hero); Body — DM Sans (clean, modern); Accent — Satisfy (script, for taglines and quotes)

**Idea B — "Island Wellness & Movement"**

- **Design Movement**: Organic minimalism with kinetic energy — inspired by Mauritian coastal lifestyle
- **Core Principles**:
  1. Warm neutral base with vibrant color pops (not flat, textured)
  2. Full-bleed photography as primary storytelling medium
  3. Generous whitespace punctuated by bold typographic moments
  4. Soft rounded shapes contrasted with sharp editorial typography
- **Color Philosophy**: Warm sand (#F2E8D9) as base, deep ocean teal (#1B7A74) as primary, sunset coral (#E8603C) as accent, leaf green (#4A7C59) for wellness sections. Palette mirrors Mauritius's natural landscape.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Split-screen hero (text left, full-bleed image right), horizontal scrolling service showcase, masonry photo grid for social proof
- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Leaf/lotus motifs as section dividers (SVG)
  2. Large pull-quote typography with thin rule lines
  3. Floating stat badges (45 likes, 9 comments) styled as social proof chips
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth parallax on hero image, horizontal scroll on mobile for services, testimonials in a looping marquee
- **Animation**: Parallax hero (0.3x scroll speed), service cards slide in from alternating sides, stats count up on viewport entry
- **Typography System**: Display — Cormorant Garamond (elegant, high contrast); Body — Nunito (friendly, round); Numbers — Bebas Neue (bold stats)

**Idea C — "Vibrant Celebration Grid" (CHOSEN)**

- **Design Movement**: Contemporary Caribbean event branding — bold, festive, community-driven
- **Core Principles**:
  1. Explosive color confidence: teal, gold, magenta as a triumvirate palette
  2. Asymmetric grid layouts that feel alive and dynamic, not templated
  3. Photography-first storytelling with text as supporting cast
  4. Tactile depth: layered cards, drop shadows, gradient overlays on images
- **Color Philosophy**: Brand teal (#2BBCB0) as primary identity color, warm gold (#F5A623) for warmth and celebration, deep magenta (#C0392B → #E91E8C) for energy and excitement, dark navy (#1A2332) for text and depth. The palette directly mirrors the Vibra TD Events logo and Aura Vibes poster.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Full-viewport hero with diagonal clip-path bottom, 3-column asymmetric service grid (one card spans 2 rows), testimonials in a horizontal scroll strip, contact section with split background
- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Diagonal clip-path section transitions (consistent 8-degree angle)
  2. Teal/gold gradient overlays on service card images
  3. Large decorative quotation marks in brand teal for testimonials
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Service cards have image zoom + overlay reveal on hover, CTA buttons have ripple effect, nav links have underline-slide animation
- **Animation**: Hero text cascades in (staggered 80ms), service cards fade-up on scroll, testimonials auto-scroll with pause on hover, contact form fields slide in from bottom
- **Typography System**: Display — Raleway ExtraBold (strong, geometric, modern); Body — Lato (clean, readable); Accent — Dancing Script (for taglines and quotes — festive, handwritten feel)
</text>
</response>

## CHOSEN: Idea C — "Vibrant Celebration Grid"
Rationale: Best matches Vibra TD Events' existing brand identity (teal/gold/magenta from logo and Aura Vibes poster), the festive energy of their events, and the tropical Mauritius context. The asymmetric grid and diagonal cuts convey movement and celebration without being chaotic.
